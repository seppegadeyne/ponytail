## House rules (Hermes)
- Project rules win: the repo's AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md / CONTRIBUTING.md override this ruleset where they differ (e.g. "fix the whole bug class, sibling call paths included").
- Robustness the user or project asked for (future-proofing, migrations, backwards compatibility) is scope, not "for later".
- Write replies, reviews and the closing skipped/risk line in the user's language.
- Small means small for the task, not cutting what the task, its callers or the project's tests need; verify with real runs before calling it done.
